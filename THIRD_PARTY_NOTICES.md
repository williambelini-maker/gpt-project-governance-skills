# Third-Party Notices and Provenance

The project licenses apply only to material for which the Project Licensor has the necessary rights.

## General rule

Do not interpret `LICENSE.md`, `LICENSE-SOFTWARE.md`, or `LICENSE-DOCUMENTATION.md` as relicensing third-party material.

Third-party materials remain subject to their own licenses, terms, rights, and attribution requirements.

This includes, where applicable:

- external software dependencies;
- copied or adapted documentation;
- quoted text;
- datasets;
- images;
- trademarks and brand assets;
- external templates;
- standards;
- academic material;
- contributed material with separate provenance.

## Contribution provenance

Any contribution containing third-party material should identify:

~~~text
SOURCE =
AUTHOR_OR_RIGHTSHOLDER =
LICENSE =
URL =
MODIFIED = YES | NO
COMPATIBILITY_REVIEW = PASS | OPEN
~~~

## No rights by reference

Linking to, naming, discussing, or referencing a third-party project, company, model, product, standard, or service does not claim ownership of that third party's intellectual property.
