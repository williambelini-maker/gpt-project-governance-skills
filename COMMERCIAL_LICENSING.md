# Commercial Licensing

Commercial rights are reserved except where an applicable public license expressly grants a particular use.

The purpose of this repository is to permit study, research, experimentation, adaptation, contribution, and other permitted noncommercial uses while keeping commercial exploitation subject to direct agreement with the rights holder.

## When to request a commercial license

Request authorization before uses such as, where they constitute commercial use under the applicable license:

- selling the Skills, methodology, templates, or derivatives;
- paid redistribution;
- incorporating covered material into a commercial product;
- using covered material as a material part of a paid SaaS, consulting, managed service, or commercial agent offering;
- monetizing a derivative or packaged version;
- commercial sublicensing or commercial distribution.

## Possible commercial terms

A commercial agreement may include one or more of:

- royalties or revenue share;
- participation in the commercial initiative;
- project contribution or upstream contribution;
- partnership or co-development;
- fixed licensing fee;
- support/maintenance arrangement;
- another negotiated form of consideration.

These are possibilities, not preset terms. No participation, royalty, fee, partnership, or other commercial condition exists unless agreed in writing.

## How to request

Contact the repository owner through the GitHub account associated with this repository.

If no private contact method is available, open a GitHub issue titled **Commercial Licensing Request** asking for a private contact channel. Do not place confidential business information in a public issue.

A request should identify:

- who intends to use the material;
- which files/Skills/materials are involved;
- the product or service;
- expected commercial context;
- whether redistribution is involved;
- desired term/scope of the license.

## No implied authorization

Discussion, evaluation, a pull request, an issue, contribution, or informal conversation does not grant commercial rights.

Commercial authorization requires a separate written agreement.
