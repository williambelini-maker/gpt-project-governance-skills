# Commercial License Agreement — Draft Template

`DRAFT_TEMPLATE_ONLY = TRUE`

This is a drafting framework. **It grants no rights unless completed and accepted in writing by the relevant parties.**

## Parties

**Licensor:** <legal name/entity>  
**Licensee:** <legal name/entity>  
**Effective date:** <date>

## Licensed materials

Identify exact materials by repository URL plus commit, tag, release, or file list.

`LICENSED_MATERIALS = <commit/tag/files>`

## Rights

Select and define as applicable:

- [ ] internal commercial use
- [ ] incorporation into a named product
- [ ] SaaS/hosted-service use
- [ ] customer distribution
- [ ] modification
- [ ] sublicensing
- [ ] other: <define>

Define exclusivity, territory, and term.

## Restrictions

Define prohibited resale, sublicensing, notice removal, misrepresentation, or use outside scope.

## Economic terms

Choose and define any fixed fee, royalty, revenue share, milestones, participation, contribution/co-development, support fees, or other consideration.

No economic participation exists unless written into the completed agreement.

## Ownership and improvements

Define ownership of the Licensed Materials, Licensee materials, modifications, improvements, and any contribution-back mechanism.

## Attribution and notices

Define required notices and any permitted modifications.

## Support / maintenance

`SUPPORT_INCLUDED = YES | NO`

If yes, define scope, targets, exclusions, and fees.

## Confidentiality

Define confidentiality terms or reference a separate NDA.

## Warranties, indemnity, liability

Define negotiated warranties, indemnities, liability caps, exclusions, and mandatory-law carve-outs.

## Termination

Define breach, cure, non-payment, scope violation, insolvency where applicable, mutual termination, and post-termination effects.

## Compliance

Define relevant export, sanctions, privacy, consumer, sector-specific, or AI-related obligations.

## Governing law and dispute forum

No repository-wide choice is imposed. A specific signed agreement may select governing law, courts/arbitration, venue, and controlling language.
