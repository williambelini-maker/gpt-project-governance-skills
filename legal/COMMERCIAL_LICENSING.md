# Commercial Licensing

Commercial rights are reserved except where an applicable public license expressly grants a particular use.

The public repository supports study, research, experimentation, adaptation, contribution, and other permitted noncommercial uses. Commercial exploitation that is not already authorized by an applicable license requires a separate written agreement.

## Typical cases requiring discussion

Depending on the applicable license and facts:

- selling covered Skills, methodology, templates, packages, or derivatives;
- paid redistribution;
- incorporating covered material into a commercial product;
- material use in paid SaaS, consulting, managed services, or commercial agent offerings;
- monetizing a derivative or packaged version;
- commercial sublicensing or distribution.

## Possible terms

A negotiated agreement may use royalties, revenue share, participation, project contribution, partnership/co-development, fixed fees, support/maintenance, or another written arrangement.

None of those terms exist automatically.

## Request channel

Contact the repository owner through the GitHub account associated with the repository.

If no private channel is available, open a **Commercial Licensing Request** issue asking for a private contact channel. Do not publish confidential business information.

A useful request identifies the requester, material involved, product/service, commercial context, redistribution needs, and desired scope/term.

## No implied authorization

Issues, pull requests, discussions, evaluation, or informal conversations do not grant commercial rights.

Commercial authorization requires a separate written agreement.

## Agreement template

A non-binding drafting template is available at `COMMERCIAL_LICENSE_TEMPLATE.md`.

## Contributions

Commercial licensing of incorporated external contributions depends on contributors having accepted `CONTRIBUTOR_LICENSE_AGREEMENT.md`.

## Governing law and disputes

The repository does not preselect one universal governing law, court, venue, or arbitration forum.

A signed commercial agreement may define those terms. Otherwise any concrete dispute remains subject to mandatory applicable law and competent authority.
