# Legal Position

`DOCUMENT_ROLE = LEGAL_POLICY_STATE`

## Current posture

```text
PUBLIC_REPOSITORY = YES
SOURCE_AVAILABLE = YES
PUBLIC_COMMERCIAL_PERMISSION = NO
OPERATIONAL_SKILL_LICENSE = POLYFORM_NONCOMMERCIAL_1_0_0
DOCUMENTATION_LICENSE = CC_BY_NC_SA_4_0
SEPARATE_COMMERCIAL_LICENSING = ENABLED
CONTRIBUTOR_CLA = REQUIRED
THIRD_PARTY_PROVENANCE_POLICY = REQUIRED
```

The project intentionally does not preselect one universal jurisdiction, forum, commercial entity structure, or dispute mechanism.

## Case-specific choices

If a specific signed agreement exists, follow it to the extent legally effective. Otherwise concrete disputes or transactions remain subject to mandatory applicable law and competent authority.

## Contribution chain

Before merging third-party contributions:

```text
CLA_ACCEPTED?
CONTRIBUTOR_AUTHORITY_CONFIRMED?
THIRD_PARTY_MATERIAL_DISCLOSED?
LICENSE_PROVENANCE_CLEAR?
-> ONLY THEN MERGE
```

## Commercial-deal gate

Before granting commercial rights:

```text
LICENSED_MATERIALS IDENTIFIED
COMMERCIAL_SCOPE DEFINED
ECONOMIC_TERMS DEFINED
SIGNING_AUTHORITY CONFIRMED
THIRD_PARTY_EXCLUSIONS REVIEWED
CASE_SPECIFIC_LEGAL_TERMS CHOSEN IF NEEDED
-> READY TO SIGN
```

Review this position when a concrete contribution, commercial negotiation, dispute, trademark/brand adoption, material third-party content, multi-jurisdiction transaction, or material economic arrangement arises.
